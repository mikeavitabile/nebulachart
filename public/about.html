About Nebula

Shape strategy together.

Nebula is a collaborative visual strategy-mapping application. It helps teams organize priorities across strategic axes and time horizons, then discuss and refine the map together in real time.

What Nebula does

  • Create visual strategy maps with rings, axes, and movable nodes.
  • Save maps securely to your account.
  • Share maps with view-only or editing access.
  • Collaborate with teammates and see changes shortly after they happen.

How sign-in works

Nebula uses Google Sign-In or passwordless email links for authentication. Google Sign-In is used only to identify you. Nebula does not request access to Gmail, Drive, Calendar, contacts, or your Google password.

Read the Nebula Privacy Policy for details about how account information is handled.
